# Incident Response Playbook

Priority 1 incidents represent a full outage, confirmed data exposure, or a security event with active customer impact.

P1 customer communication expectations:
- Initial customer-facing acknowledgment within 15 minutes.
- Executive escalation within 20 minutes.
- Public status page update within 30 minutes when customer-facing services are affected.
- Incident commander assigned immediately and bridge opened within 10 minutes.

Service restoration targets:
- Containment plan within 30 minutes.
- Recovery plan within 60 minutes.
- Written post-incident review within 3 business days.
