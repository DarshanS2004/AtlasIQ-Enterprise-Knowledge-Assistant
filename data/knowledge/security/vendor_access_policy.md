# Vendor Production Access Policy

Vendors may access production systems only when the following controls are in place:

1. An approved statement of work and business owner.
2. A completed security assessment rated medium risk or lower, or a documented exception signed by the CISO.
3. Named accounts with hardware-backed MFA. Shared accounts are prohibited.
4. Access limited by time window, IP allow-list, and just-in-time approval workflow.
5. Session logging retained for at least one year.

Emergency access must expire within four hours and requires after-action review within one business day.
